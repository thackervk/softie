# Softie

A pixel platformer about Softie the red panda, who travels the world and then blasts off into space, dodging lava and spikes.

**Play it:** https://thackervk.github.io/softie/

## How to play
- **Phone/tablet:** ◀ ▶ to run, **JUMP** (tap for a small hop, hold for a big jump), **BOOM** for an extra-high blast jump once per jump. Tap **PAD** to show or hide the touch buttons.
- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys or A/D to run, Space/W/Up to jump, X or Shift to BOOM, R to restart, P to pause.
- Touch a wall in mid-air and press jump to wall-jump.

## Features
- 27 levels in three parts:
  - **World Trip (1–16):** Auckland's Sky Tower, the Eiffel Tower, Big Ben, the Pyramids, Sydney Opera House, Mt Fuji, the Statue of Liberty, Mt Ngauruhoe, Samarkand's Registan, the Ram Mandir in Ayodhya, the Statue of Unity in Gujarat, a giant Hanuman statue by a river in India, Diwali night in Varanasi, Holi in Vrindavan, Kedarnath temple and Dubai's Burj Khalifa. Each level ends at a passenger jet from a made-up airline (Kiwi Wings, Softie Air, Crown Jet, Desert Star Airways, Sunrise Pacific, Blossom Air, Skyline Air, Volcano Express, Silk Road Air, Saffron Skies, River Breeze, Hanuman Air, Diya Airways, Rangoli Air, Mahadev Air and Golden Dune Air) that takes off with Softie on board, and a flight board shows where Softie is flying next.
  - **Space Trip (17–25):** unlocked by beating Rooftop Run (level 16), then each level unlocks the next. You can also skip ahead with coins. Places: a launch site, the Moon, a moon crater, the asteroid belt, Mars, Europa, Saturn's rings, a space station and the Sun. Low gravity, floating rocks, and a rocket at the end of every level that blasts off when Softie climbs in. Beating a space level flies Softie straight to the next one. Every space level has a different rocket, drawn in the style of real rockets from different space agencies and companies (Saturn V, Soyuz, Falcon 9, Starship, New Shepard, Electron, Atlas V, LVM3, Ariane 5, Long March 5 and H3 styles), without their logos.
  - **Wormhole (26–27):** Wormhole Gate (in front of a glowing orange black hole) and Through the Wormhole (in front of a bright white hole with jets). Step into a swirling wormhole and Softie pops out of the one with the same color, to get past walls, lava and empty space.
- Lava, spikes, wall-jumps, crumbling blocks, rising lava and wormholes.
- Coins for every level beaten, plus a shop with abilities (Speed Paws, Spark Magnet, Cool Lava, Mega BOOM, Double Jump), fur colors and accessories.
- Original music, including a handpan track and chant-style tunes on traditional Hindu mantras for the Ayodhya, Hanuman, Diwali, Holi and Kedarnath levels, with sound effects. Music can be switched off with ♪.
