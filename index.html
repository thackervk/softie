Softie

Softie the red panda travels the world, from Auckland's Sky Tower through Samarkand, Ayodhya, the Statue of Unity, a giant Hanuman statue, Diwali, Holi and Kedarnath to Dubai's Burj Khalifa, flying between stops, then blasts off into space to the Moon, Mars, Saturn and through a wormhole (space levels are unlocked with coins). Dodge the lava, skip the spikes and bounce up the walls to reach every door and rocket.

Beat Volcano Core to fly to space! Each level you beat unlocks the next. You can also skip ahead in space with coins.

Phone: hold ◀ ▶ to run, tap JUMP for a small hop, hold it for a big one. BOOM blasts Softie extra high once per jump. Keyboard: arrows or A D, Space to jump, X to boom, R restart, P pause. Tap ♪ to change the music and PAD to show or hide the touch buttons.
